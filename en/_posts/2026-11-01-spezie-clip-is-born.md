Spezie Clip is online: a shop for spices, herbs and blends in resealable pouches with a clip.

## What you will find

Four ranges: spices, herbs, peppers and chillies, blends and seasonings. Every product is available from 5 g at €0.99, or in 10, 25 or 50 g. Each page explains how to use and store it.

## What is coming

On the blog we publish guides on storing and using spices. If you have a request, write to us from the Contact page.
