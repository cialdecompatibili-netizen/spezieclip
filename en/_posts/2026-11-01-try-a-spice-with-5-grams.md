Before buying a big jar of a spice you do not know, there is a simpler way: try a little.

## Why start with 5 g

Five grams are enough for a few recipes and cost €0.99. If you like the spice, next time choose 10, 25 or 50 g.

## How to choose the weight

- **5 g**: to try a new spice.
- **10 g**: for occasional use.
- **25 g**: for spices you use every week.
- **50 g**: for basics, such as pepper, paprika and oregano.

## After opening

Always close the pouch with the clip: the spice lasts longer and smells better.
