Spices and dried herbs lose aroma mainly because of light, heat and moisture. A few habits make the difference.

## Close well after every use

With the pouch with a clip, just close it right away: air and kitchen humidity stay out.

## Away from the stove

Heat and steam are the worst enemies. A pantry or drawer is better than the shelf above the cooker.

## Always a dry spoon

Never measure spice directly over a boiling pot: steam gets into the pouch. Use a dry spoon.

## Whole or ground

Whole spices, such as peppercorns and nutmeg, keep their aroma longer than ground ones: grind them fresh.

## When to replace them

Note the opening date. If you smell little when opening the pouch, it is time to replace the spice.
